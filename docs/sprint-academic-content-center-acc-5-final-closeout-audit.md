## Foreign-domain ownership and lifecycle

The foreign domains retain their own mutation paths. Curriculum, CurriculumUnit and CurriculumLesson use `softDeleteCurriculum`, `softDeleteUnit` and `softDeleteLesson` in `src/modules/academics/curriculum/infrastructure/curriculum.repository.ts`. LessonPlan and LessonPlanItem use `softDeletePlan` and `softDeleteItem` in `src/modules/academics/lesson-plans/infrastructure/lesson-plans.repository.ts`. HomeworkAssignment has its own cancellation/status path in `src/modules/homework/application/homework-assignments.use-cases.ts`; GradeAssessment has `softDeleteAssessment` in `src/modules/grades/assessments/infrastructure/grades-assessments.repository.ts`. Timetable owns a hard `deleteEntry` in `src/modules/academics/timetable/infrastructure/timetable.repository.ts`.

ACC Preparation/Subject Resource references to Curriculum, Unit, Lesson and Lesson Plan/Item, and Weekly Plan joins to Homework and Assessment, are composite same-school FKs with `ON DELETE RESTRICT`. They preserve historical links when a foreign row is soft-deleted and do not cascade deletion _from ACC into_ a foreign domain. Timetable references in Preparation and Online Session are UUIDs without an ACC FK. That permits the Timetable owner to hard-delete an entry; ACC retains the historical ID. `validateTimetable` rejects adoption on a new or changed detail, while the repository's exact normalized no-op returns before revalidation. These topologies are intentionally different; ACC does not take deletion ownership, clear foreign rows, or synchronize their lifecycles. Existing Curriculum soft-delete verification covers the FK-backed class; the new Timetable test covers the non-FK hard-delete class without duplicating that case.
