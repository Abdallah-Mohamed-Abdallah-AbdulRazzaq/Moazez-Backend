## Media/DR07 provider and security acceptance

[ST03 capability request](../assessment-media/st03-runtime-capability-request.md), [ADR-0017](../../adr/ADR-0017-assessment-media-foundation.md), [media policy](../../src/modules/files/assessment-media/domain/assessment-media.policy.ts), [runtime policy](../../src/modules/files/assessment-media/application/assessment-media-runtime.policy.ts), [verifier](../../src/modules/files/assessment-media/application/assessment-media-verifier.ts) and [upload contract](../../src/modules/files/assessment-media/domain/assessment-media.contract.ts) govern this boundary.

* Runtime uploads default off; the runtime policy unconditionally denies Production. Missing School policy/categories/actor flags deny. `UnapprovedAssessmentMediaSecurityGate` always rejects; no environment flag selects a fake scanner. A separate approved source/release decision is needed for future Production enablement.
* Generic Files remains 10 MiB. Assessment ceilings are 10 MiB non-video and 200 MiB video, intersected with stricter current School/technical policy. Proposed larger document/audio sizes are not accepted. Candidate MIME registry: text/plain, PDF, JPEG/PNG, MPEG/MP4/WebM audio and MP4/WebM video. Actual support is the intersection of policy, technical inspection and security acceptance; signature recognition alone cannot release PDF/images or universal content.
* School policy caps include 8 references/resource, 2 concurrent uploads, 30 requests/hour, bounded resource/School bytes and duration/dimensions. Policy maxima are not launch settings or evidence of scanner capability. Technical Learning Media constraints can be tighter (3,600 seconds, 1,920×1,080), even where assessment policy's absolute dimension cap is 4,096. Question-bound answers exclude video and cap configured active files at 3 (default 1). Same-parent verified stimulus reuse charges physical storage once while reference/resource limits still apply.
* Application intent TTL is 30 minutes; nominal issued capability TTL is 15 minutes, but the actual provider capability/session expiry is authoritative and persisted. DevOps must prove expected length/MIME enforcement, exact headers/origin bounds, create-only staging identity, maximum provider resumable lifetime, replay behavior and CORS. An application cancellation cannot be assumed to revoke an already issued bearer capability.
* MinIO's exact-size POST policy is isolated evidence. Real GCS resumable initiation/length enforcement, signed-origin behavior, keyless signer restrictions, generation/version/ETag and immutable server-owned final objects require actual provider proof. A storage adapter's existence is insufficient.
* Verification compares actual object metadata/identity before and after bounded reads, validates bytes/hash/MIME, checks technical duration/dimensions and requires server-owned CLEAN attestation matching checksum/version. Current assessment deadline is 90 seconds and at most two verifier flights/process. Timed-out opaque SDK work retains its slot until actual settlement; no false cancellation/released-capacity claim is made. Target CPU/memory/temp disk/network deadlines/concurrency must support this behavior.
* DevOps/Security must propose and obtain approval for the scanner/provider, malware verdicts, unavailable/timeout/retry handling, full-content coverage, quarantine location/access/lifecycle and attestation authenticity. Do not create or activate paid services or quarantine infrastructure under ST14. No provider success is claimed from the synthetic test attestor.
* [Private transport](../../src/infrastructure/storage/private-media-content.service.ts) and [response transport](../../src/infrastructure/storage/private-media-content.response.ts) enforce current resource authorization, trusted inline types, safe attachment disposition, private/no-store/nosniff, GET 200/206/416, authorized HEAD 200 and abort handling. Verify actual provider Range and storage abort/late-settlement behavior and allowed application/storage origins. Neither signed capability nor object coordinates may enter logs or client long-term state.
* [File Lifetime](../../src/modules/files/shared/infrastructure/file-lifetime.repository.ts) globally retains current and governed historical references/claims, including soft-deleted assessment links. Existing source has 30 physical File relations / 29 retained consumers, with upload provenance excluded. ## Proposed eligible-target execution and rollback checklist

1. **Read-only discovery**: DevOps produces current eligible-target, data/retirement, artifact, IAM, provider, budget, secret-version, network, probe, backup and monitoring evidence. Owner/Planning/Security resolve missing authorities before any mutation.
2. **Target-specific authorization**: Owner approves the exact isolated environment, costs, tested categories, operational scope and resource limits. Separate governed source/provisioning work is required if current guards do not admit that target. Retired Staging remains a hard stop.
3. **Rehearsal plan**: DevOps/Backend approve migration and compatibility rehearsal, actual client journeys, role/queue exclusivity, private bytes/Range/abort, scanner outage/negative content, key approval/release/revocation, sync audit rollback, failure injection and bounded load. Use synthetic owned data only.
4. **Governed execution if separately authorized**: bind approved artifact and data/backup references; perform the target-eligible migration/verified-role/readiness/smoke sequence. Keep media disabled until its distinct DR07 gate is approved. No command in this document authorizes execution.
5. **Success evidence**: current authenticated HTTP/body/byte assertions, exact DB/custom-SQL/privilege state, compatible role/queue behavior, configured probes/alerts, resource/latency budgets, client/device receipts and reviewed cleanup. Explicitly label FAIL/BLOCKED/NOT_RUN rather than promoting a partial proof.
6. **Failure/rollback**: stop later promotions; drain owned work and preserve logs/receipts. DevOps/Owner select a still-compatible immutable runtime artifact, forward migration or approved isolated restore under the data-authority checkpoint. Never run automatic down migrations, reset a shared DB, replay a consumed plan, restore ambiguous email side effects, overlap schedulers or consume a reserve as rollout capacity.
7. **Cleanup**: delete only the separately approved owned disposable fixture resources after label/identity/path/retention checks and capture zero remaining resources. Preserve task worktrees, evidence, migration bytes, Production objects and unknown operational artifacts. File deletion is not QA cleanup authority for business data.

Required recipients: independent DevOps lead (target/release/capacity/probes), Security/Owner (DR07/provider/quarantine/identity), Backend Engineering (source/migration/API/transaction compatibility), Client owners (real apps/accessibility) and Planning/Product (budgets and final acceptance).
