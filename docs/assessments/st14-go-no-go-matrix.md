NOT_CERTIFIED means the available evidence cannot support certification. NOT_APPLICABLE requires a source-backed reason, not missing work.
* Source requirements and denied security classes remain accepted PASS from ST13. They do not certify actual clients or operational categories. The [unchanged requirement/coverage matrices](st14-final-program-closeout.md#requirement-closure) preserve their denominators and F-12 provenance.

Focused checks and normal build remain distinct evidence. Those limitations must appear in the independent review decision; ST14 documentation does not silently accept them on an Owner's behalf.

Current locking/authorization/atomic audit protections stay intact. A later approved budget and eligible-target load evidence are required for operational performance acceptance.
